# Priority Production-AI Role Brief

Use this generic checklist for current and future senior AI-platform interviews:

- Python/backend engineering and distributed systems.
- Production LLM applications, tool calling and agent/runtime orchestration.
- RAG, semantic search, embeddings, context/memory and hallucination mitigation.
- Evaluation, guardrails and AI observability.
- Local/open-source model serving, GPU inference, batching and capacity.
- Docker/Kubernetes, CI/CD and cloud-native deployment.
- Reliability, performance, latency/throughput/cost optimization.
- Technical leadership and clear architecture trade-offs.
